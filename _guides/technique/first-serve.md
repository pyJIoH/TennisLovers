## Grip

Hold the racket as if you were holding a hammer: the **continental** grip. It feels odd at first, but it is the grip every good serve is built on.

## Stance

Stand side-on to the net, front foot pointing toward the net post, back foot parallel to the baseline.

## The toss

Release the ball at eye height with a straight arm and let it rise just above your full reach, slightly in front of you. If the toss is wrong, catch it and start again.

## Rhythm

Both arms go down together and come up together. Reach up to the ball and let the racket swing through toward the target.

## Drills to practise

1. **Toss only.** Toss and let the ball drop; it should land just in front of your front foot.
2. **Service line serves.** Serve from the service line first, then step back once it feels easy.
