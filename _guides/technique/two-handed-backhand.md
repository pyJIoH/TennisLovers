## Grip

The bottom hand (right hand for right-handers) holds a continental grip; the top hand sits above it in an eastern forehand grip.

## Preparation

Turn your shoulders early, with both hands on the racket, until your chin is near your front shoulder.

## Swing and contact

Swing low to high and make contact beside your front hip. Think of it as a left-handed forehand (for right-handers) helped by the bottom hand.

## Common mistakes

- **Letting go too early.** Keep both hands on until after contact.
- **No shoulder turn.** Without the turn, the shot becomes all arms.
