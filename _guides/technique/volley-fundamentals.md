## Grip and ready position

Use the continental grip so you do not need to change grips at the net. Hold the racket up and in front of your chest.

## The punch

Turn your shoulders slightly, keep the racket head above your wrist, and punch forward through the ball. There is almost no swing.

## Footwork

Step in with the opposite foot (left foot for a right-hander's forehand volley) to add weight to the shot.
