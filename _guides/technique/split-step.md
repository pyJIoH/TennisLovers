## Why it matters

The split step loads your legs so you can react in any direction. Without it you start every shot flat-footed.

## Timing

Leave the ground slightly before your opponent makes contact, and land as you see where the ball is going.

## Drills to practise

1. **Call and go.** A partner points left or right as you land; push off in that direction.
2. **Rally with a split step.** Rally and say "hop" out loud every time your partner hits.
