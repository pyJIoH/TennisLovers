## Let go

After the point, turn your back to the net, walk to the back fence and take one slow breath.

## Plan

Pick one simple idea for the next point, such as "deep cross-court" or "first serve to the body".

## Ready

Bounce the ball or adjust your strings the same way every time, then step up and play.
