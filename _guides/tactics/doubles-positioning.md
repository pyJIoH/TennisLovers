## Up and back

One player at the net, one at the baseline. It is the usual starting position for most club players, but it leaves a gap in the middle.

## Both at the net

When both players reach the net, the team controls the point. Follow a good approach shot or serve to the net together.

## Covering the middle

Agree before the match who takes the middle ball. A common rule: the player with the forehand in the middle takes it.
