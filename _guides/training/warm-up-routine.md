## Movement

Two or three minutes of light jogging, side shuffles and skipping to raise your heart rate.

## Dynamic stretches

Arm circles, leg swings and walking lunges. Save long, static stretches for after you play.

## On court

Start with mini tennis from the service line, move back to the baseline, then hit a few volleys, overheads and serves.
