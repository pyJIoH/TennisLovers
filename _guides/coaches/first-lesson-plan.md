## Structure

1. **Welcome and warm-up.** Ball games and movement.
2. **Grip and ready position.** Eastern forehand grip, ready stance.
3. **Drop-feed forehands.** Coach feeds from close range.
4. **Mini rally.** Coach and player rally in the service boxes.
5. **Wrap-up.** One thing to remember and practise.
